intent to basket · a quick commerce experiment

Say the meal.Get thebasket.

Instead of searching for what you need, tell the store what you want to achieve. Type “dinner for 4 under ₹600” and the app plans the meal, scales it to the head count, maps every ingredient to a real product, counts the packs, fits the budget and hands you a basket you can edit and check out.

Concept build. Prices and packs are modelled on real Indian quick commerce SKUs; delivery, stock and payment are simulated.

Aisle 01 the core flow

Six stops from a sentence to a basket

Every step is a real artefact in the codebase, not a prompt and print.

Intent

intent.ts

Reads dish, occasion, head count, budget, diet and constraints from the sentence.

Plan

planner.ts

Chooses the dishes, prices each candidate against the catalogue, spends the budget on the best menu that fits.

Quantities

recipes.ts · units.ts

Per serving amounts scaled linear, sub linear or fixed. Converts units, including per piece weights.

Products

matcher.ts

Ranks real products for each ingredient by key match, pack fit, waste, rating and price.

Budget

planner.ts

Cheaper packs first, then drops optional ingredients, then explains any shortfall honestly.

Basket

basket.ts

Orchestrates it all and applies the shopper's swaps and removals.

natural language → intent → meal plan → scaled ingredients → real products + pack counts → editable basket → cart → checkout

Aisle 03 the kitchen maths

Cooking for ten is not ten times the oil

Three ways to scale

Multiple of one serving, by head count
  • Linear: rice, chicken, curd
  • Sub linear (servings to the 0.65): oil, pastes, herbs
  • Fixed: one pack of masala for 2 or 10

How a product wins

Each candidate is scored per ingredient in matcher.ts
  • ▲Carries the ingredient key (closest key ranks highest)
  • ▲Its name says the ingredient label back
  • ▲Rating and number of ratings
  • ▼Pack waste (a 5 kg sack for 400 g)
  • ▼Each extra pack needed
  • ▼Cost, weighted harder in cheapest mode
Per piece weights: “2 onions” can be bought from a 1 kg pack.

When the budget bites

The planner tries, in order
  1. Swap to cheaper packsSame ingredient, lower cost.
  2. Drop optional ingredientsSaffron goes before the rice does.
  3. Explain the shortfallHonestly, in plain language.
